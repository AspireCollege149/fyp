How to Enroll

Joining Aspire is simple. Follow these steps to secure your place.

1

Check Eligibility

Review program-specific eligibility and required documents on our admissions page.

2

Fill Online Form

Complete and submit the online application form with accurate personal and academic details.

3

Entry Test

Appear for the college's entry test or submit your Board/BISE result for merit-based selection.

4

Confirmation

Receive admission confirmation, pay your fees, and collect your student ID to begin classes.
